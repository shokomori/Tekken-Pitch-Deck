About the Tournament

A GRASSROOTS TEKKEN 8 EVENT
FOR PAMPANGA

Pampanga Powerhouse is a passionate grassroots community of fighting game players based in Angeles City, Pampanga. We organize community-driven events that welcome first-time competitors and seasoned veterans alike.

Eligibility

Open to everyone. Beginners and experienced players are both welcome to join.

Registration

Early Bird registration is ₱400 and Walk-in registration is ₱450. Both include a tournament slot, 1 raffle entry, and a Player ID.

Schedule

Early Bird / Pre-Registered: August 8, 10:00 AM to 11:30 AM. Walk-ins: August 8, 11:00 AM to 11:30 AM. No advance payment is required.

Tournament Format

DOUBLE ELIMINATION
BEST OF 3 UNTIL TOP 8

The event uses double elimination, best of 3 during elimination rounds, and best of 5 for Top 8. Tournament settings follow the event default setup for competitive consistency.
