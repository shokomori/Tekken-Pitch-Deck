About the Tournament

A GRASSROOTS TEKKEN 8 EVENT
FOR PAMPANGA

Pampanga Powerhouse is a passionate grassroots community of fighting game players based in Angeles City, Pampanga. We organize community-driven events that welcome first-time competitors and seasoned veterans alike.

What This Is

A one-day Tekken 8 tournament built to bring local players together in a competitive but welcoming environment.

How to Join

Register through the form, scan the QR code, and check in on-site during the registration window on August 8.

What to Expect

Clear entry fees, an open bracket, tournament rules, controller guidelines, and a well-organized community event flow.
